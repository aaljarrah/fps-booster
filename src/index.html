What these mean

  • Average FPS — overall smoothness. Higher is better.
  • 1% Low — the average of your slowest 1% of frames. This is what stutter feels like; raising it matters more than average.
  • 0.1% Low — worst-case hitching. Big gaps here = noticeable freezes.
  • Stutter (frametime σ) — frame-to-frame consistency. Lower is smoother.
