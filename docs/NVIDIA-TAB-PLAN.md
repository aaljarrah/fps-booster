# FrameForge — NVIDIA Tab Expansion Plan (judged)

> Produced by a 14-agent workflow (6 research streams, 3 design proposals, 4-lens judge panel, synthesis). Binary-verified against the bundled nvidiaProfileInspector 2.4.0.31.

## Concept
FrameForge "NVIDIA" tab — a Preset-first shell (judge-winning Design A) with Design B's per-setting Inspector folded in as the "Advanced (per-setting)" drawer, plus two Design-C primitives grafted on: the per-row "Measure it" PresentMon A/B hook and the hard G-SYNC-detection gate on the Vsync-Force-On path. Three honest preset cards (Esports / Balanced[default] / Quality) lead; every preset is split into two visually distinct halves — APPLIED driver settings FrameForge actually writes via a generated .nip + -silentImport, and a DO-IT-IN-GAME advise checklist (Reflex On, Reflex 2/Frame Warp, MFG=OFF) that FrameForge cannot toggle and never fakes. A persistent status strip gates every Apply behind a confirmed DRS restore point. The tab is brutally honest for THIS rig (i9-14900KF + RTX 5080 @ 1080p/241Hz, CPU-bound): the only real click-to-photon win (in-game Reflex) is not a driver toggle, so presets cannot masquerade as a complete fix; driver levers are framed as consistency/insurance, not FPS; and the placebo cluster is quarantined read-only with per-item reasons.

## Winning direction
HYBRID per the judge panel's explicit recommendation: "Ship Design A as the default surface, and fold Design B's per-setting tab in as the 'Advanced (per-setting)' drawer behind it — they are complementary, not competing." Design A won the UI/UX lens (9) for best fit with FrameForge's dual simple/advanced model and best trust/learnability; Design B supplies the impact-badge vocabulary and always-visible Placebo drawer that power A's drawer (tied-best on Safety/Honesty, 9). Design C won the Benefit/Evidence (9) and Safety/Honesty (9) lenses but its full per-game master/detail IA was judged too heavy for v1; we steal its two highest-value primitives regardless of shell: (1) the per-row "Measure it" PresentMon A/B hook — the only mechanism across all three that PROVES a gain or honestly reports "within margin of error", and (2) the code-level hard G-SYNC-detection gate on the Vsync-Force-On stack. Per-game profiles (C's core) are kept as a Phase-5 roadmap item, not v1. A's one-click risk (shipping an unverified ID behind a single button) is neutralized by making live ID/value resolution + round-trip-verify a HARD precondition for every written setting, including preset items.

## UI / UX
LAYOUT — three vertical zones inside the NVIDIA tab.

A green check means a full Drs-folder snapshot exists; absent = every Apply button disabled with a single CTA "Create restore point first". Right: "Active: Balanced (applied 2h ago)" / "Custom" / "Stock". Far right: a small green shield "Driver config - not injection - ban-safe (VAC/Vanguard/EAC/BattlEye)".

[2] PRESET CARDS (hero, 3 across). Each card = identity line, one "what this is for" sentence, an HONEST qualitative impact chip (never a fabricated ms/FPS number), the headline diff, one primary button, and a "See exactly what changes" link opening a DIFF SHEET (Setting | current value [live] | after | revert-to [labeled NVIDIA-default, never a false 'prior value'] | why + impact-tag + source link | SettingID on hover). Button label is literal: "Apply Balanced (writes N verified driver settings)" where N counts only settings whose ID/value were live-verified on this driver — unverifiable settings are dropped from the count and shown disabled with "couldn't verify on your driver". After apply the card flips to "Applied (check) - Revert".
  - ESPORTS / LOWEST LATENCY: "Tear-tolerant, absolute-minimum input lag (what many CS2/Valorant pros run)." Chip: "Latency: best - Tearing: yes - Avg FPS: unchanged."
  - BALANCED (default, "Recommended" ribbon): "Tear-free and within ~1ms of the latency floor." Chip: "Latency: ~1ms above Esports - Tearing: none - Consistency: best." The G-SYNC + Vsync-Force-On combo here is HARD-GATED on positive VRR detection; if VRR not confirmed the card auto-degrades to the Esports sync model and says so.
  - QUALITY: "For heavier titles (Squad/PUBG/Apex high) when GPU-bound." Chip: "Visual stability over raw latency - behaves like Balanced on your 1080p shooters." Explicitly touches NO image-quality knobs.

[3] DO-IT-IN-GAME PANEL (directly under the cards, NOT hidden — the honesty centerpiece). Per-game checklist FrameForge cannot toggle: Reflex Low Latency = On (CS2/Valorant/Apex/Fortnite/PUBG/Dota/DBD/League; note "Squad has no Reflex - Balanced's cap covers it"); Reflex 2 / Frame Warp = On (Valorant, The Finals; RTX-50 only; "minor edge-warp on fast flicks" caveat); red WARN row "DLSS Multi-Frame Generation = OFF for competitive - raises the FPS number, ADDS latency." Each row has a user-ticked checkbox (persisted, self-checklist only) and a "Copy steps" button. Above-fold honesty banner: "On your CPU-bound 1080p rig the headline FPS number will not move. The real wins are in-game Reflex + your sync choice; driver settings here buy frametime consistency and correctness."

ADVANCED (per-setting) DRAWER (collapsed; this is Design B). Per-setting rows grouped Latency / Sync & Cap / Power / Shader / Image-Quality, each with: friendly name, IMPACT BADGE from a fixed vocabulary [REAL LATENCY (green) / REAL CONSISTENCY (teal) / IMAGE-QUALITY TRADEOFF (amber) / INSURANCE-CORRECTNESS (blue) / SITUATIONAL-FALLBACK (grey) / PLACEBO-LEAVE-DEFAULT (red, disabled)], live current-value chip (or "unknown - will verify on apply" where unreadable), recommended value starred, plain-English mechanism, rig-aware live-impact note, a "Why / Evidence" expander with source links, the verbatim SettingID (hover), and a per-row "Revert this" + "Measure it" button. Editing anything sets active preset to "Custom (based on Balanced)". The guided SYNC/CAP MODEL CHOOSER lives here as a 2-option radio (A: Vsync Force Off + uncapped + Reflex; B: G-SYNC + Vsync Force On + sub-refresh cap — gated on VRR) so users can never hand-assemble the VSYNC back-pressure trap; an "edit raw sync rows" disclosure exposes the individual toggles for experts.

PLACEBO DRAWER ("Cargo-cult tweaks we deliberately don't ship - tap for why") — visible in BOTH Simple and Advanced (honesty is not an advanced feature). Read-only, controls disabled, each with one-line debunk + SettingID: Texture Filtering=High Performance, Aniso Sample Opt, Trilinear Opt, Threaded Optimization=Off (flagged potentially HARMFUL on the 8P+16E 14900KF), driver AA/MSAA/AO override, CUDA Force P2=Off, Negative LOD Bias (with anti-cheat-scrutiny security note).

MICROCOPY ETHOS: qualitative impact tags only; no fabricated numbers; a "Measure it" button on every measurable row deep-links the Benchmark tab for a PresentMon A/B (frametime mean, 1%/0.1% lows, click-to-photon proxy) that will and must report "within margin of error on your rig" when true, stamping the result back onto the row.

## Presets
### Esports / Lowest Latency
Tear-tolerant, absolute-minimum input lag (the config many CS2/Valorant pros run). Latency best, tearing yes, average FPS unchanged.

- APPLIED: Vertical Sync = Force Off (per-game on detected competitive titles)
- APPLIED: Power Management Mode = Prefer Maximum Performance (per-game; framed consistency-not-FPS)
- APPLIED: Low Latency Mode = Off (in-game Reflex does it better; Ultra can hurt CPU-bound)
- APPLIED: Preferred Refresh Rate = Highest available (insurance for the 241Hz panel)
- APPLIED: Shader Cache: verify ENABLED + size large/Unlimited (anti-stutter hygiene, not a gain)
- APPLIED: Background Application Max Frame Rate = 30 (alt-tab power/heat QoL, not FPS)
- APPLIED: no driver frame cap (run uncapped; let in-engine/Reflex manage the queue)
- DO-IT-IN-GAME: Reflex Low Latency = On per supported game
- DO-IT-IN-GAME (WARN): DLSS Multi-Frame Generation = OFF
- Every APPLIED row is written only after live ID/value verification; unverifiable rows are skipped and shown disabled

### Balanced (Recommended, default)
Tear-free and within ~1ms of the latency floor - the right default for clean enemy tracking. HARD-GATED on positive G-SYNC/VRR detection; degrades to Esports sync model if VRR is not confirmed.

- APPLIED (VRR-gated): G-SYNC Compatibility = Enabled (only if live VRR detected)
- APPLIED (VRR-gated): Vertical Sync = Force On (Blur Busters low-lag-Vsync; safe ONLY with VRR + sub-refresh cap)
- APPLIED: sub-refresh cap deferred to in-game Reflex auto-cap (~225 at 241Hz); driver Max Frame Rate ~234 written ONLY for Reflex-less titles (Squad)
- APPLIED: Power Management Mode = Prefer Maximum Performance (per-game)
- APPLIED: Preferred Refresh Rate = Highest available
- APPLIED: Shader Cache: verify ENABLED + large/Unlimited
- APPLIED: Background Application Max Frame Rate = 30
- DO-IT-IN-GAME: Reflex On (its auto-cap normally sits under the VRR ceiling); Reflex 2/Frame Warp where offered
- DO-IT-IN-GAME (WARN): DLSS Multi-Frame Generation = OFF

### Quality
For the heavier titles (Squad/PUBG/Apex high) when you are GPU-bound. On the 1080p flick-shooters it behaves like Balanced. Touches NO image-quality knobs.

- APPLIED: same tear-free sync stack as Balanced (G-SYNC + Vsync Force On + sub-refresh cap), VRR-gated
- APPLIED: Power Management Mode = Prefer Maximum Performance (per-game)
- APPLIED: Preferred Refresh Rate = Highest available
- APPLIED: Shader Cache: verify ENABLED + large/Unlimited
- NOT TOUCHED: Texture Filtering Quality left at Quality (degrading it is placebo on a 5080@1080p)
- DO-IT-IN-GAME: Reflex On; DLSS Super Resolution = per-game advise ONLY for GPU-bound heavier titles
- DO-IT-IN-GAME (WARN): DLSS Multi-Frame Generation = OFF for competitive

### Stock / Revert (always present)
Honest layered revert. Because the bundled NPI has no headless export, exact prior-value capture is unavailable headlessly - so reverts are labeled truthfully.

- Tier 1 (surgical, no reboot): write each FrameForge-touched setting back to its recorded intended value or to the documented NVIDIA default via revert.nip + -silentImport, labeled 'restored to NVIDIA default' (NOT a false 'your prior value')
- Tier 2 (full rollback, needs reboot): restore the complete Drs-folder snapshot (incl. nvdrswr.lk) + reboot - the authoritative exact rollback
- Tier 2 is also the recovery path if Tier 1 -silentImport returns Access-Denied (the 2025 OTA/DRS-lock failure mode)

### Why (per setting)
- **NVIDIA Reflex Low Latency (in-game)** — The single biggest real click-to-photon win for this rig and the one FrameForge CANNOT flip - it is an engine flag, so it ships as an ADVISE checklist row, never a fake toggle. Reflex drains the render queue in-engine and overrides driver Low Latency Mode. Largest when GPU-bound; small at CPU-bound 1080p but zero downside. On vs On+Boost differ only when GPU-bound, so plain On is the honest default.
- **Power Management Mode = Prefer Maximum Performance** — Pins the GPU to top P-state so it can't downclock-stutter when a CPU-bound 1080p title barely loads it. A frametime-CONSISTENCY win (steadier 1% lows), NOT more average FPS - must never be sold as an FPS gain. Applied per-game to avoid idle desktop power/heat. Decimal derived from hex at runtime (research's 274806641 is WRONG). Default is Optimal Power=5 on modern drivers; record the TRUE live default so 'restore to default' is honest.
- **Vertical Sync** — Dominant sync lever. Force Off = lowest latency, tears. Force On is safe ONLY inside G-SYNC + Reflex/sub-refresh cap (Blur Busters low-lag stack) and is HARD-GATED on live VRR detection - forcing it without VRR is the classic back-pressure trap. The Force-On value code is contested across sources (0x2046BCE4 vs 0x138F2648 vs 0x47814940); route through verify-before-write and exclude from one-click presets until confirmed against the bundled NPI 2.4.0.31 enum.
- **G-SYNC Compatibility** — The substrate the tear-free stack rests on. The only G-SYNC ID confirmed in the bundled overlay. Gate the Vsync-Force-On preset path on a positive VRR-capability detection using this ID; the VRR app-override family (0x10A879CF/0x10A879AC) is unverified and suspiciously close to the Vsync ID - do not wire it.
- **Low Latency Mode (driver NULL / ULL)** — Reflex overrides it; it does nothing in DX12/Vulkan and can mildly hurt when CPU-bound (the normal state here). Shipped as advise/verify defaulted Off, not an action. NOTE the separate 0x0005F543 (dec 390467) is the COSMETIC NVCP tracking flag ('no need to change it') - NEVER write it as a functional lever; one research entry wrongly recommends it.
- **Driver Max Frame Rate (NVCP limiter)** — Last-resort cap: in-engine cap < RTSS ~ driver cap for latency. Auto-DISABLED for Reflex titles (Reflex auto-cap is lower-latency); reserved for Squad. The limiter ID is genuinely ambiguous (0x10834FEE control vs 0x10835002 value vs 0x1083500A NVCPL); the catalog must disambiguate control-vs-value and round-trip-verify which the active driver honors before writing.
- **Background Application Max Frame Rate = 30** — Caps an alt-tabbed game's FPS for power/heat/noise; ZERO effect on the focused game's latency or FPS. Ship as safe QoL, never as an FPS gain. Decimal derived from hex (research's 277041174 is WRONG).
- **Preferred Refresh Rate = Highest available** — Insurance/correctness only: forces the panel's 241Hz max if a game requests a lower mode. On a correctly-configured panel most titles already pick max refresh, so changes nothing measurable - badge INSURANCE, not a win. Decimal derived from hex (research's 6599489/6600081 disagree).
- **Shader Cache - enabled + large size** — Anti-stutter HYGIENE (fewer shader recompiles), not FPS. Cache is already on/~1GB by driver default, so the honest action is VERIFY-not-disabled - badge 'verify/hygiene', not an applied gain. The size knob (0x00AC8497) and the enable flag are two different settings the research conflates; treat separately and verify both. The 'uncertain' 0x00BD8FCA should be dropped.
- **Texture Filtering - Quality** — PLACEBO on this rig: CPU-bound at 1080p on a 5080 means no fill-rate to save; 'High Performance' yields ~0 FPS and adds shimmer that can hurt target acquisition. Quarantined read-only in the Placebo drawer with this reason.
- **Threaded Optimization** — PLACEBO/HARMFUL: forcing Off is cargo-cult from the dual-core era and can REDUCE FPS in CPU-bound DX11 titles on an 8P+16E 14900KF. Quarantined read-only and explicitly flagged potentially harmful.
- **CUDA - Force P2 State** — PLACEBO for gaming: measured no significant average-FPS change; only restores VRAM clocks for CUDA workloads with mixed frametime results and mild CUDA-use risk. Quarantined; never sold as an FPS booster.
- **Texture Filtering - Negative LOD Bias** — Image-stability knob with ZERO FPS effect; 'clarity/sharpening' LOD tweaks have historically drawn unfair-advantage scrutiny in some titles. Kept OFF the actionable list entirely; shown only in the Placebo drawer with a security note.

## Apply / revert model
VERIFIED PREMISE CORRECTION (binary-confirmed): the bundled resources\npi\nvidiaProfileInspector.exe (2.4.0.31, Jan-15 build) CLI surface is exactly -applaunch, -cl, -createCSN, -disableScan, -showOnlyCSN, -silent, -silentImport. I confirmed by extracting .NET UTF-16 user strings: silentImport/silent/createCSN/disableScan/showOnlyCSN/applaunch are PRESENT; exportCustomized has 0 occurrences. Export is GUI-only (btnExport, frmExportProfiles, exportUserdefinedProfilesToolStripMenuItem). Therefore the research/proposals' headless -exportCustomized baseline is UNBUILDABLE and is removed from the design.

APPLY (real, verified): generate frameforge_apply.nip from the catalog - one <Profile> per detected competitive game keyed by exe in <Executeables> for per-game settings, touching 'Base Profile' (_GLOBAL_DRIVER_PROFILE) ONLY for genuinely-global settings (Preferred Refresh insurance, Shader Cache, Background-App cap) with a loud UI flag. SettingID + Dword SettingValue are written as DECIMAL, derived at RUNTIME from authoritative hex (never the research's contradictory decimals - several are wrong: PREFERRED_PSTATE=274197361 not 274806641; FRL_FPS=277041154; BkgFRL=277041158; PrefRefresh=6600001). Apply via elevated `nvidiaProfileInspector.exe -silentImport "<path>"` from FrameForge's existing elevated PowerShell engine; capture exit code + NPI log as the success signal. DRS writes commit immediately; effect on NEXT game launch - NO reboot. UI says "Close and relaunch the game."

BASELINE / DETECT (corrected, no headless export): (1) FULL Drs-folder snapshot to data\state\nvidia-snapshots\<ts>\ - this is the AUTHORITATIVE baseline and the only guaranteed exact rollback. Engine must snapshot the WHOLE folder including nvdrswr.lk (the current engine omits it - bug to fix). (2) Run -createCSN to emit CustomSettingNames.xml at the user's exact 576+ driver version for the authoritative SettingID->name/value-enum map (used to resolve and verify IDs the research flags 'likely/uncertain'). We do NOT parse nvdrsdb*.bin directly (fragile, ruled out).

LIVE VERIFICATION (HARD precondition for every write, including preset items): resolve each ID/value via -createCSN; round-trip write-then-confirm where feasible. Any setting whose ID/value cannot be confirmed on the live driver renders DISABLED ("couldn't verify on your driver - not applying") and is excluded from the preset count - never blind-written.

REVERT (layered, HONEST about its limit): Tier 1 (surgical, no reboot) writes each FrameForge-touched setting back via revert.nip + -silentImport to its recorded intended value or the documented NVIDIA default, labeled "restored to NVIDIA default". CRITICAL HONESTY: because there is no headless export, we CANNOT capture the user's exact prior customized value, so Tier 1 must NOT claim "restored to your prior value" - that would be the fake precision FrameForge forbids. Tier 2 (full rollback, needs reboot) restores the complete snapshotted Drs folder and reboots - the TRUE exact rollback and the source of the reversibility guarantee. Never do partial single-.bin restores (per-app nvdrsdb1.bin restores silently fail). On Tier-1 Access-Denied (2025 OTA/DRS-lock), do not retry blindly - steer to Tier 2 (reset Drs folder + reboot). The .nip format has no unset/reset token and the CLI cannot reset, which is exactly why Tier 2 snapshot-restore is the backbone, not an afterthought.

## Detection model
Capture-baseline, never blind 'assume-unknown', upgraded from the current engine\nvidia.ps1 -Action detect. Report: NVIDIA DRS present (Test-Path Drs), tuner present, snapshot count (already there) PLUS driver branch/version (registry/WMI, for the '576+/RTX-50' gating), live display refresh (241Hz) + resolution (1080p) + G-SYNC/VRR capability (gates the Vsync-Force-On preset path), and the -createCSN-derived SettingID->name/enum map for the user's driver. Because headless value export does NOT exist in the bundled NPI, current-value chips in the Advanced drawer are sourced from the -createCSN map + best-effort live read; where a value cannot be read, the chip is labeled 'unknown (will verify on apply)' rather than guessed.

Game detection (drives per-game profiles + the in-game checklist): scan Steam (libraryfolders.vdf -> appmanifest_*.acf: CS2 730, Dota 570, PUBG 578080, DbD 381210, Squad 393380), Epic (Fortnite), Riot (Valorant + League), EA (Apex), with a manual 'Add by .exe' fallback. Map each to the canonical exe NPI writes into <Executeables> (cs2.exe, VALORANT-Win64-Shipping.exe, r5apex.exe, FortniteClient-Win64-Shipping.exe, TslGame.exe, dota2.exe, DeadByDaylight-Win64-Shipping.exe, 'League of Legends.exe', SquadGame.exe). Reflex-coverage + API map drives fallback logic: only offer driver Low Latency Mode / Max Frame Rate where a game BOTH lacks Reflex AND is DX9/DX11 (none of the core list qualifies, so the panel mostly just instructs Reflex On; Squad auto-flagged 'no Reflex -> relies on cap'). Detection is advisory: an undetected game does not block applying the Base Profile.

## Safety gates
- HARD GATE: no Apply button enabled until a FULL Drs-folder snapshot exists (status-strip green check). Snapshot must include nvdrsdb0.bin, nvdrsdb1.bin, nvdrssel.bin AND nvdrswr.lk - the current engine omits the lock file; fix before ship or Tier-2 rollback is not bit-exact.
- HARD GATE (code-level, not prose): the Vsync = Force On / G-SYNC tear-free preset path is enabled ONLY on a positive live VRR/G-SYNC-compatibility detection (via verified ID 0x109DB0D3 / dec 278769875). Without it, FrameForge refuses Force-On and steers to Vsync Force Off - forcing Vsync On without VRR + sub-refresh cap is the back-pressure trap, the opposite of low latency.
- HARD GATE: live ID/value verification (-createCSN map + round-trip) is mandatory for EVERY written setting, including one-click preset items. Any unverifiable ID/value renders disabled and is excluded from the preset; never blind-write the research's contradictory decimals/value codes.
- NEVER write 0x0005F543 (ULL CPL tracking flag, dec 390467) as a functional lever - it is cosmetic per NPI's own description; one research entry wrongly recommends it.
- Honest revert labeling: Tier-1 reverts are labeled 'restored to NVIDIA default' (not 'your prior value'), because the bundled NPI cannot export the user's exact prior value headlessly. Record the TRUE live default per setting (e.g. Power Management default = Optimal Power 5, not 0) so default-restore is correct.
- Placebo cluster (Texture Filtering=High Performance, Aniso Sample Opt, Trilinear Opt, Threaded Optimization=Off [flagged HARMFUL on 8P+16E], driver AA/MSAA/AO override, CUDA Force P2=Off, Negative LOD) kept OUT of all applied presets, in a read-only drawer with per-item reasons.
- Negative LOD Bias and any 'strip textures/effects below the game's own minimums / see-through-smoke' behavior are permanently off the actionable list - that is the one genuine unfair-advantage (not injection) anti-cheat risk in this domain. FrameForge ships nothing that does this and says so.
- Anti-cheat posture surfaced persistently: DRS/NvAPI writes via NPI are configuration (same store as NVCP/MSI Afterburner), NOT injection or game-memory tampering - ban-safe across VAC, Vanguard, EAC, BattlEye. The NVIDIA tab is visually/architecturally separated from FrameForge's process-affinity/priority tweaks (the actual kernel-anti-cheat risk).
- Driver Max Frame Rate is auto-DISABLED for Reflex titles (Reflex auto-cap is lower-latency) and reserved for Reflex-less Squad; resolve the limiter ID ambiguity (0x10834FEE control vs 0x10835002 value vs 0x1083500A NVCPL) and verify which the live driver honors before writing.
- Reflex / Reflex 2 / DLSS-MFG-OFF are ADVISE-only checklist rows, never fake driver toggles; expectation set up front that headline FPS will not move on this CPU-bound 1080p rig.
- Interrupted-apply handling: verify -silentImport exit code; on any failure offer immediate Tier-2 snapshot restore rather than leaving a half-applied profile set (higher risk as per-game profile count grows).
- Base Profile (global) writes are minimized and loudly flagged; per-game profiles preferred so the desktop/browser are unaffected and revert is cleaner.

- **Phase 4 - Measure-it A/B (Design C primitive)** — Per-row 'Measure it' button deep-linking the Benchmark tab (PresentMon: frametime mean, 1%/0.1% lows, click-to-photon proxy) for single-setting A/B; stamps an honest verdict back onto the row, including 'within margin of error on your rig'. This is the anti-snake-oil capstone.
- **Phase 5 - Per-game profile manager (Design C IA, optional/future)** — Promote the Advanced drawer toward C's Base + per-game-override master/detail view with inheritance badges and pre-existing-profile no-clobber detection, once the catalog/detection are proven. Kept out of v1 for cognitive-load reasons per the UI/UX judge.

## Open risks
- No headless value export in the bundled NPI means FrameForge cannot capture the user's exact prior values; Tier-1 surgical revert can only restore documented NVIDIA defaults, and the only guaranteed exact rollback is the Tier-2 snapshot-restore + reboot. The UI must state this honestly; users wanting exact prior-value undo must use Tier 2.
- Several latency-critical SettingIDs/value codes are 'likely/uncertain' on driver 576+ and the research corpus contradicts itself (Vsync ID and Force-On code, LLM functional-vs-tracking flag, the frame-cap ID 0x10834FEE vs 0x10835002 vs 0x1083500A, Power Mgmt default 0 vs 5). The live-verify gate mitigates but means some settings may render disabled until confirmed on the user's actual driver.
- -createCSN may not emit a complete value-enum for the very newest 576+ settings, limiting how fully FrameForge can resolve/verify some IDs headlessly; a setting that can't be verified is dropped from presets, which can make a preset write fewer settings than advertised.
- The 2025 NVIDIA OTA DRS-clobber / Access-Denied failure mode can force users into the heavier Tier-2 reboot path and, in rare locked states, require deleting the Drs folder + reboot.
- Game/exe detection is launcher-specific and will miss titles, requiring the manual exe-picker fallback; a missed game means its per-game profile and in-game checklist row are absent.
- Honesty has a marketing cost: on this CPU-bound 1080p rig the genuine wins are consistency/insurance, not headline FPS, and much of the tab is advise + measurement rather than one-click action - which may underwhelm users expecting a big FPS number. This is the correct-but-less-marketable outcome.
- An interrupted -silentImport across multiple per-game profiles can leave a partial state; the design mitigates with exit-code checks and immediate Tier-2 offer, but per-app nvdrsdb1.bin partial restores are unreliable, so recovery still implies a reboot.

## Judge summary
The panel split cleanly: Design A won UI/UX (9) and Reliability (6, by smallest write blast radius), Design B and C tied on Safety/Honesty (9), and C won Benefit/Evidence (9) for its measure-it hook. The verdict was an explicit hybrid: ship A's preset-first shell, fold B's per-setting tab in as the Advanced drawer, and steal C's two best primitives (per-row PresentMon A/B + the hard G-SYNC gate). The single most important cross-cutting finding - which I binary-verified - is that the bundled nvidiaProfileInspector 2.4.0.31 has NO -exportCustomized flag (its real CLI is -silentImport/-silent/-createCSN/-disableScan/-showOnlyCSN/-applaunch), so the 'exact prior-value, surgical, no-reboot revert' that all three proposals sold is unbuildable headlessly; the design now treats the full Drs-folder snapshot (which must include nvdrswr.lk, a fix to the current engine which omits it) as the authoritative baseline and labels Tier-1 reverts honestly as 'restore to NVIDIA default'. The second load-bearing finding, also verified: the bundled Reference.xml is a custom-overlay containing only the 0x1083xxxx + 0x109DB0D3 IDs - every standard latency ID (Vsync, PowerMizer, LLM, FRL, MPRF) is absent and must be resolved/verified live via -createCSN, and the research's hardcoded DECIMALS are frequently wrong (PREFERRED_PSTATE is 274197361 not 274806641; FRL_FPS 277041154; BkgFRL 277041158; PrefRefresh 6600001). Hence live ID/value verification is a HARD precondition for every write, including preset items; unverifiable settings render disabled. The plan keeps Reflex/Reflex2/MFG as advise-only, quarantines the placebo cluster (flagging Threaded-Optimization-Off as harmful on the 8P+16E), keeps Negative LOD off the actionable list, gates the Vsync-Force-On stack on real VRR detection, and is brutally honest that on this CPU-bound 1080p rig the wins are consistency/correctness, not headline FPS.
